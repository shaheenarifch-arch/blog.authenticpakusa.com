How do you choose the right size?

US sizes often fit a little larger than local sizing, so measure a sweater that already fits well and compare it before you order.

  1. Lay a sweater that fits you well flat on a table.
  2. Measure straight across the chest, from armpit to armpit, and double it.
  3. Send the measurement and the person's usual size to us on WhatsApp, and we'll suggest the closest US size.

How can you tell a genuine Polo Ralph Lauren sweater from a fake?

Look at the details: a cleanly embroidered pony logo, neat and even stitching, a smooth-running zip, and proper neck and care labels.

  • The pony logo: genuine embroidery is crisp, with a clear horse, rider and polo mallet. Fakes often look blurry or lumpy.
  • Labels: a neatly stitched neck label and a care label with fabric content and washing instructions.
  • Zip and ribbing: a smooth zip and even ribbed cuffs and hem.
  • Price: a price far below US retail plus shipping is the biggest warning sign.

How do you look after a Polo quarter-zip?

Follow the care label inside the sweater. In general: wash gently in cold water, zip it closed first, and dry it flat.

  1. Read the care label first: fabric content varies by style (cotton, wool or blends), and the label gives the right instructions for that exact sweater.
  2. Close the zip and turn it inside out before washing, so the zip doesn't snag the knit and the pony logo is protected.
  3. Use a gentle, cold cycle or hand-wash with a mild detergent. Hot water can shrink knitwear.
  4. Dry it flat on a towel, away from direct sun. Hanging a wet sweater can stretch the shoulders.
  5. Fold, don't hang, for storage to keep the shape through the season.
Cream and light colours: wash them separately from dark clothes, especially the first few times.
