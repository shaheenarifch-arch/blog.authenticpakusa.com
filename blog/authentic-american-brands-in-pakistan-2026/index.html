Quick answer

To buy authentic American brands in Pakistan, order from a seller that imports directly from the USA and will show you real photos of the exact item. Before you pay, check batch codes and expiry dates on makeup, perfume and supplements, verify serial numbers on electronics, and be wary of prices far below US retail.

  • Ask for close-ups: batch code, expiry date, serial number or box label.
  • Verify it yourself: Apple serials on Apple's Check Coverage page, cosmetics batch codes on a batch checker.
  • Confirm the details: current price, delivery time and returns, in writing.
  • Shop the real thing: Authentic Pak USA imports genuine US brands and takes orders on WhatsApp.

Key takeaways

  • A price far below US retail is the single biggest warning sign.
  • Genuine products carry codes (batch, lot, serial) that match their box and can be checked.
  • Expiry dates matter most for skincare, baby care and supplements.
  • A trustworthy seller sends real photos of the exact item before you pay.

Why are fake US brands so common in Pakistan?

Fakes are common because popular American brands are expensive to import, so copies, repacked items and expired stock can be sold at prices that look like bargains.

Whether you want MAC foundation, a Nike sneaker or a pair of AirPods, the online listings you'll find range from genuine imports to convincing copies. The most common problems are:

  • Copies (replicas): made to look like the original, often with slightly wrong fonts, colors or packaging.
  • Repacked or refilled items: real-looking bottles and boxes with different contents inside.
  • Old or expired stock: genuine products that are past their best, a real risk for skincare, baby care and supplements.
  • Mismatched electronics: devices whose serial number doesn't match the model, or isn't registered with the brand at all.

How do you spot fake American products?

Check the codes that genuine brands print on every product: batch codes on cosmetics and perfume, lot numbers and expiry dates on supplements, and serial numbers on electronics and luxury goods, then confirm they match the box.

Quick checks you can do yourself

  • Apple products: enter the serial number on Apple's Check Coverage page. A genuine device shows its model and warranty status.
  • Cosmetics and perfume: look up the batch code on CheckFresh to see when it was made.
  • Sneakers: the style code on the inside tag (for example on Nike shoes) should match the code on the box label exactly.

What should you check before you order?

Before you order, confirm the exact item, its current price, the delivery time to your city, and how returns work, and ask for photos of the codes that prove it's genuine.

  1. The exact variant: shade, size, model or color. Most returns come from ordering the wrong one.
  2. Photos of the codes: batch code, expiry date or serial number of the item you'll receive.
  3. The full price: confirm it includes everything, so there are no surprises on delivery.
  4. Delivery time: imported items can take longer than local stock; ask for an estimate for your city.
  5. Returns: ask what happens if the item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered.
Tip: for skincare, baby care and supplements, ask for the expiry date before you pay. Genuine stock with a long shelf life is worth waiting for.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to buy American brands online in Pakistan?

It can be, if the seller imports directly from the USA, shows real photos of the product, and answers questions about batch codes, expiry dates and serial numbers before you pay. Avoid listings that refuse close-up photos or price premium brands far below normal.

How can I check if makeup or perfume is original?

Compare the batch code printed on the product with the code on the box, check that the packaging is sealed and the print is sharp, and look up the batch code on a cosmetics batch checker to see when it was made.

How do I check if AirPods or an iPhone are genuine?

Enter the serial number on Apple's official Check Coverage page. A genuine device returns its model and warranty status; a fake or cloned serial usually returns an error or a different model.

Why are authentic US products more expensive than local listings?

Genuine imported products carry the US retail price plus shipping and import costs. A price far below that is the most common sign of a fake, a repacked item or an expired batch.
